[h1]The Definitive Multiplayer Mod[/h1]
Multiplayer QoL for Terraria on tModLoader. Aimed to replace Better Multiplayer with far more configurable features.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Auto team join[/b]: assign a host-chosen team when players join the world
[*][b]Boss fight lives[/b]: per-player or per-team respawn limit
[*][b]Death-only spectate[/b]: teammates and bosses via hotkeys and a head grid, with smooth camera
[*][b]Respawn at spectate target[/b]: during boss fights, respawn next to the teammate you were watching
[*][b]Instant respawn when the boss ends[/b]: clears death timers
[*][b]Unlimited team teleport[/b]: map-click teleport to teammates without Wormhole Potions
[*][b]Shared team health mode[/b]: optional shared HP pool (always on, or bosses only), with a configurable pool multiplier
[*][b]Custom death screen[/b]: separate copy for slain, out of lives, and team wipe
[*][b]Boss fight stats feed[/b]: live party heads, view teammates' HP, damage dealt/taken, deaths
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish
