[h1]The Definitive Multiplayer Mod[/h1]
Multiplayer QoL for Terraria on tModLoader. Aimed to replace Better Multiplayer with far more configurable features.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Auto team join[/b]: put joining players on a host-chosen team
[*][b]Free teammate teleport[/b]: map-click teleport without Wormhole Potions (optional boss block)
[*][b]Shared team health mode[/b]: one team HP bar, pool multiplier, avg regen, uncapped heart bar
[*][b]Player swap mode[/b]: every X minutes (default 5), living teammates randomly switch places
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]Player life limit[/b]: cap how many times each player can die in a fight
[*][b]Team life limit[/b]: cap total team deaths (stacks with player limit)
[*][b]Hard lock[/b]: out of lives means you stay dead until the boss ends
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]Damage you take / deal[/b]: host sliders, 0.5×–3× (1.0 = normal)
[*][b]Boss max health[/b]: scale boss HP at spawn, same range
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]Death-only spectate[/b]: teammates and bosses via hotkeys and head grid
[*][b]Custom death screen[/b]: slain, out of lives, and team wipe copy
[*][b]Boss stats feed[/b]: party heads, HP, damage dealt/taken, deaths
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]Respawn wait[/b]: host base seconds instead of vanilla difficulty timers
[*][b]Timer add-ons[/b]: per-player, boss/event mults, escalate per boss death
[*][b]Health and mana on respawn[/b]: percent of max when you come back
[*][b]Respawn beside teammate[/b]: during bosses, next to who you were watching
[*][b]Instant when the boss ends[/b]: death timers clear as the fight ends
[/list]

[h2]Config[/h2]
Server Config is editable by any player (handy on cloud/dedicated hosts). Client Config covers spectate and the stats feed.

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish
