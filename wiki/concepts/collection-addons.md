Add-ons for a **BestMultiplayer co-op collection**. These stay separate mods — multiplayer-specific, not general QoL (no Magic Storage / Recipe Browser / etc. here).

## Primary add-on

| Mod | Workshop | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared World Map** | [`2815010161`](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2815010161) | Syncs exploration between players (live / region / full share + request). Core co-op exploration gap vanilla never fills. Do **not** reimplement in BestMultiplayer. |

## Collection notes

- BestMultiplayer owns team join, unlimited team TP, boss lives, death spectate UI.
- Shared World Map owns map fog sync; host/dedicated config can be finicky — document for players, don’t absorb.
- Other multiplayer-ish mods are [acknowledged](catalog-ack.md) only unless promoted later.
